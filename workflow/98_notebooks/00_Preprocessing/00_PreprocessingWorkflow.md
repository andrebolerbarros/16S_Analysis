# 16S Analysis: Pre-processing


## 3. Decontamination

Identifying contaminants in our biological samples is a challenge. There are several options available:

* `decontam`
* **`SCRuB`** (Source tracking for Contamination Removal in microBiomes): Probabilist *in silico* decontamination methods that takes information across multiple samples & controls to identify and remove contaminants
  * Paper: [Contamination source modeling with SCRuB improves cancer phenotype prediction from microbiome data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-023-01696-w)]  
  * Repository/Tutorial: [SCRuB Tutorial](https://shenhav-and-korem-labs.github.io/SCRuB/)
